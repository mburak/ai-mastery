A 24-week hands-on learning path from experienced software engineer to AI-era engineering leader.

## Structure

Each phase has its own directory with projects, experiments, and notes:

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Directory |
|-------|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | 1-4 | Foundations & Modern Tooling | `phase-1-foundations/` |
| 2 | 5-8 | How AI Actually Works | `phase-2-neural-nets/` |
| 3 | 9-12 | LLM Engineering & Prompt Mastery | `phase-3-llm-engineering/` |
| 4 | 13-16 | RAG, Embeddings & Knowledge Systems | `phase-4-rag/` |
| 5 | 17-20 | AI Agents & Autonomous Systems | `phase-5-agents/` |
| 6 | 21-24 | Production AI & Technical Leadership | `phase-6-capstone/` |

## Blog Posts

Drafts and published posts live in `blog-drafts/`. One post per phase, published on completion.

This repo documents my journey returning to hands-on engineering after 5 years of management, now focused on building deep AI expertise. Every project here was built to learn, not to demo.
